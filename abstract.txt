Minecraft is an open-world sandbox game featuring, among other things, sheep husbandry. Sheep grow wool and consume grass, meaning the rate at which a pasture produces wool is tied to the rate at which grass can regrow. We model grass and sheep populations with deterministic and stochastic approaches. We find that certain sheep populations maximize wool production, with the exact population depending on how often the sheep are sheared. However, if the population is too high, the farmer risks depleting the pasture's grass.
